About Koomapatti

A quiet treasure in Virudhunagar district, where tradition meets tranquility

Where Roots Run Deep

Koomapatti is a peaceful village located in Virudhunagar district, Tamil Nadu. The village is known for its rich agricultural heritage, predominantly cultivating paddy (rice) with the blessing of the Pilavakkal Dam's water resources.

The community here is warm and closely-knit, celebrating Tamil festivals with great enthusiasm. From Pongal harvest celebrations to temple festivals, every occasion is marked with colour, music, and unity.

Surrounded by hills, fields, and reservoirs, Koomapatti offers a rare slice of rural Tamil Nadu life — unspoiled and authentic.

Sowing Season

Farmers begin sowing in June–July, carefully transplanting paddy seedlings into flooded fields, marking the start of the agricultural year.

☀️

Growing Season

The lush green fields transform under the Tamil Nadu sun. Village life revolves around tending the crops, singing folk songs while working.

🌾

Harvest – Pongal

January brings the joyous Pongal harvest festival — a time of thanksgiving to the sun and earth, celebrated with kolam, music, and feast.

💧

Pilavakkal Waters

The dam's canal network is the lifeline of agriculture here, ensuring water reaches every field through a centuries-old irrigation system.
